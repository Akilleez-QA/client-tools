Export names/ordinal RVAs were mapped from its actual PE export table, imagebase0x21100000. Raw disassembly and header dump remain private and excluded from publication.

The AIL_lock/AIL_unlock exported entry points at RVAs0x18a0/0x18b0 directly increment/decrement the same global counter atomically and return. They are distinct from AIL_lock_mutex/AIL_unlock_mutex at0x18c0/0x18d0, whose paths conditionally wait/release an OS synchronization object. Thus an earlier proposed owner-thread mutex interpretation of AIL_lock is not supported by this artifact. What all counter readers do, and whether an in-flight callback can outlast lock entry, needs fuller caller analysis; no drain/barrier guarantee follows.

Registration exports at0x17930 (sample) and0x1c120 (stream) call the shared internal mutex-entry path before reading/replacing a per-object callback and take its release path afterward. The stream path can also install/remove an internal sample EOS wrapper in one observed object mode. This is static control-flow observation, not a complete callback-selection or termination proof. A peer is independently checking mapping and interpretation; agreement on the same DLL remains one evidence family.
