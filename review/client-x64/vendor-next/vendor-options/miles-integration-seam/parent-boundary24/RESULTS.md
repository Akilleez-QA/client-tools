# Parent attribution check: Miles-shaped startup facade

The parent read the public facade, both implementations, the sample, adapter tests,
native receipt and portable result. `check.py` additionally matched all 33 frozen
inputs to the current local sources, checked the archived source members, audited
the two publication archives and checked the exact eight unresolved Miles imports
reported by the native compile. It also required five AMD64 objects, successful
compile/symbol commands, unchanged inputs, and no native link or execution.

This is an attribution and recorded-result check, not independent execution of
the native compile or a vendor/runtime acceptance test. The native receipt and
portable result hashes are in `results.json`.

The first two invocations exposed mistakes in this audit script: the staging
archive's `revision4-tools/` prefix maps to the local `live-bridge-candidate/`
directory, and the publication whitelist initially omitted text `.sha256` files.
Both were corrected without changing candidate source or worker evidence. The
third invocation passed. No claim relies on these failures as candidate tests.

The startup-zero path still has no qualified graceful close. The sample also
exercises the earlier component-test sequence rather than SWG's complete
`Audio::install()` policy. Provider fallback, file callbacks, serve, Bink binding
and full audio lifecycle remain integration requirements. No pipe proxy may be
passed to the existing Bink real-driver interface.

A targeted official-source lookup on 2026-09-30 (`site:radgametools.com
"AIL_startup" "return"` and `site:radgametools.com "AIL_shutdown"`) found the
[vendor development history](https://www.radgametools.com/msshist.htm).
Its old multiple-startup/single-shutdown notes do not specify the 7.2a
zero-startup cleanup contract. They are not grounds for calling shutdown after
a failed startup or treating abandonment as graceful completion.
